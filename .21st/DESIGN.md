# Portfolio design context

## Purpose

Help a recruiter, technical lead, or product/design team identify Marc, understand his strongest work, verify it, and reach the CV or contact route in under two minutes.

## Direction

- Experience mode: “Product Proof Lab”, a measured editorial archive of real work.
- Default graphite field `#070806` with a mineral identity sheet, sparse inherited orbit/star atmosphere, and rare inspection signals.
- Barlow Condensed for authored scale; Manrope for multilingual clarity.
- Lime `#d8ff3e` marks primary action and live evidence; cobalt supports product/system depth; orange is reserved for Fichestu and signal states.
- Light theme uses darker text accents to preserve contrast while keeping bright signal surfaces.

## Project system

- Three selected projects only: Klime, ATLAS, and Fichestu.
- A sticky evidence index makes comparison fast on desktop, marks the current project, and visualizes reading progress; it becomes a two-by-two jump index on mobile.
- Each project uses a distinct composition, never a repeated equal card.
- Real project titles sit inside the media plates at display scale; the active evidence rail changes signal color by project.
- Authentic covers, Marc’s actual contribution, three bounded proof points, stack, status, and working links stay visible.
- Klime leads with its complete panoramic fashion image and a separate evidence sheet; ATLAS places its identity mark on a pale cobalt field; Fichestu exposes client/backend/documentation routes.

## Interaction rules

- Preserve the morphing bubble cursor on fine pointers and the atmospheric background; a low-opacity pointer spotlight may reinforce the inspection metaphor.
- CV preview and language flags always use normal blending and retain original colors.
- GSAP progressively enhances a complete static layout; reduced-motion disables authored movement.
- Motion follows a calibration grammar: register, scan, resolve, release. The hero scans once; project reticles and evidence resolve with local scroll; trajectory and the top rail report reading progress.
- Supporting reveals are short and one-shot. Ambient motion remains limited to the inherited orbits and bubble cursor, never added to text or controls.
- One lime capability rail closes the hero and moves only with scroll, reusing the existing three proof points.
- Normal vertical flow, no modal or carousel, no horizontal overflow, no hover-only evidence.
- Portrait and document images are non-draggable and non-selectable.

## Content rules

- Spanish, Catalan, and English remain equivalent.
- No invented metrics, clients, production outcomes, dashboards, or device mockups.
- Capabilities use three staggered horizontal scanner rows with numbered proof skills, never equal cards; experience uses six alternating records with oversized chapter numerals; education becomes a full-width two-by-two mineral ledger with a cobalt lead record.
- Contact opens with a compact availability signal bar before the lime email surface.
- Education ends with a compact `SMX → DAW → DAM → IA` learning path; the CV specimen bar exposes direct ES, CA, and EN PDF links.
- The localized CV offers a keyboard-operable visual/ATS selector; preview, language links, open, download, and trilingual pack always follow the selected format.
- Visual and ATS editions are intentionally separate: the visual edition preserves the supplied Canva source in each language; ATS stays single-column, current, and machine-readable.
- The CV image keeps its original pixels and blend mode; cobalt belongs only to the surrounding summary surface.
