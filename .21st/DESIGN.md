# Portfolio design context

## Purpose

Help a recruiter or technical lead understand Marc’s work in under two minutes, then open a product, repository, technical document, CV, or contact path.

## Direction

- Experience mode: the work leads; interface recedes.
- Editorial, left-aligned composition with generous spacing.
- Default dark surface `#0b0d10`; warm light alternative.
- Blue is a signal for links, current context, and key phrasing—not decoration.
- Space Grotesk for display and Plus Jakarta Sans for body copy.

## Content rules

- Four selected projects: Klime, ATLAS, Vortex, and Fichestu.
- Each case states what it is, Marc’s contribution, three pieces of evidence, and useful links.
- Spanish, Catalan, and English remain equivalent.
- No repeated role positioning, generic process explanation, or unverified production claim.

## Interaction rules

- Normal vertical document flow; no pinned horizontal rail or inner card scroll.
- One hero entrance plus small hover feedback.
- Visible focus, 42 px minimum controls, and reduced-motion support.
- Mobile navigation remains usable without horizontal page overflow.
